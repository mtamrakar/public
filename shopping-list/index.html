My Simple Shopping List

The easiest way to manage your grocery and shopping lists

About

My Simple Shopping List is a clean, easy-to-use app that helps you organize your grocery and shopping trips. No clutter, no complexity — just a fast and simple list that works.

Features

  • Quickly add and remove items from your shopping list
  • Organize items by category
  • Simple and clean interface — no learning curve
  • Works offline — no internet connection required
  • Lightweight and fast
